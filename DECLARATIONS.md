# Declarations

Everything outside our own code that Omnikart uses. Required by the Megathon rules.

## Services and APIs

| Service | Used for | Where in the code | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AnuvaadHub** (organisers' replacement for Bhashini) | Speech recognition (ASR) in English, Hindi, Telugu | `assistant/anuvaad.ts`, `app/api/asr` | WAV, about 20 seconds, under 5 MB |
| | Machine translation (MT) between English and Hindi, English and Telugu | `assistant/anuvaad.ts`, `app/api/mt` | 50 words per call; we send at most 45 and split by sentence |
| | Text to speech (TTS) in English, Hindi, Telugu | `assistant/anuvaad.ts`, `app/api/tts` | 30 words and no special characters; we send at most 25 words, cleaned |
| **Groq** (free tier) | The LLM: picks one typed action per turn; answers one product question from that product's facts | `assistant/groq.ts`, `app/api/agent`, `app/api/ask`, `app/api/compose` | Models `openai/gpt-oss-120b`, fallback `openai/gpt-oss-20b`. The AI may also reword English sentences (`app/api/compose`); a rewrite is dropped unless every number and name is unchanged. |
| Browser: Web Audio, MediaRecorder, SpeechSynthesis, Vibration | Recording, sounds, fallback voice, phone vibration | `assistant/client/*` | No data leaves the browser except to our own `/api` routes |
| WhatsApp click-to-chat link (`wa.me`) | "Ask someone to check this cart" | `store/share.ts` | A plain link; we call nothing |

**Key pooling.** We rotate several keys per service (10 Groq keys, 4 AnuvaadHub token sets) to spread load and survive rate limits (`assistant/keypool.ts`). Key values are read from environment variables only and are never logged or sent to the browser.

**OCR.** Not used. AnuvaadHub OCR was not approved for us and needs a white background. Product photos and size charts are described from catalogue text, by hand.

## What the AI is and is not allowed to do

- It picks **one action from a fixed list** (`assistant/agent/schema.ts`). Anything else is rejected.
- It never writes a price, rating, date, stock count or total. Those come from `data/products.json` through plain code.
- It cannot place an order. The only ways are the spoken phrase (a yes plus an order word, after the read-back finished) or the labelled button (`assistant/agent/checkout.ts`).
- Review and description text is data, never instructions.
- Free answers use only that product's facts, and any number in the answer must exist in those facts or the answer is dropped (`assistant/server/facts.ts`).
- No medical, legal or financial advice.

## Data

- `data/products.json`: 12 demo products with prices in rupees. **Invented for this hackathon.** No real brands.
- Reviews in that file: **synthetic.** Generated by `scripts/build-catalogue.ts` from a seeded random generator and phrase banks, so the same command gives the same file.
- "Notes from shoppers with disabilities" (3 for each of the 3 hero products): **written by hand** as examples of the feature. They are not quotes from real people.
- Hindi and Telugu text: drafted by the assistant that helped build this project; to be read by a native speaker.
- Product pictures: simple SVG drawings generated by `scripts/build-catalogue.ts`.

## The store is a separate app

`store/` and `assistant/` share only `contract/` (types and event names). The store sends data, never HTML; the assistant has no product,
category, filter, URL or element id of its own and learns them from the store. Brand names in the demo catalogue are used for a realistic demo;
models, specifications, prices, reviews and seller names are illustrative. The cart and orders are kept in a cookie set by the store's own API.
